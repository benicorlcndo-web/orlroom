experience

PKL — Architectural Drafting & Building Documentation

during my vocational internship, I worked on architectural and building documentation projects. My work included floor plans, site plans, existing-condition measurements, and basic 3D modeling.

i worked with residential houses, villas, security posts, school buildings, and renovation projects. This experience helped me become more familiar with technical drawings, measurements, and architectural details.

through these projects, I improved my workflow with AutoCAD, SketchUp, and Enscape. The detailed project records are available in the archive.
